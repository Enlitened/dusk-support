Dusk Support

Dark mode for every website in Safari, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Turn Dusk on

iPhone and iPad: in Safari, tap the page menu in the address bar, then Manage Extensions, and turn on Dusk. Then tap the page menu, then Dusk, and choose Always Allow ▸ Always Allow on Every Website.

Mac: open Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Extensions and turn on Dusk. Then in Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Websites ▸ Dusk, choose Allow for other websites.

The Dusk app's Set up section shows these steps and turns green once Safari reports Dusk is on.

A site looks wrong

Open Dusk from Safari's toolbar (or the page menu on iPhone and iPad) on that site and choose "This site looks wrong". Filter mode darkens it a different way and usually fixes it. You can also turn Dusk off for that site, or send a report: it opens an email with the details we need.

Dusk Pro

Dark mode is free. Dusk Pro is one purchase that unlocks it on iPhone, iPad and Mac: more themes, schedules, per-site tuning, warmth and saturation, image and video dimming, dark PDFs, custom CSS and iCloud sync. If Pro isn't unlocked on a device, open Dusk ▸ Dusk Pro ▸ Restore Purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account.
