Privacy Policy

Dusk: Dark Mode for Safari · Updated 28 October 2026

Dusk doesn't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or servers of our own. Everything Dusk does happens on your device.

Websites you visit

Dusk is a Safari extension that changes the colors of web pages. To do that it reads each page's style sheets and colors on your device. It doesn't read or keep the text you read or type, your form entries or your browsing history, and it sends nothing about the pages you visit to anyone. When a page loads a style sheet or an SVG image from another site, Dusk fetches that same file again, without your cookies, so it can theme its colors; that request goes only to the site the page itself uses.

Your settings

Your settings (theme, schedules and the sites you've tuned or turned off) are stored on your device. With Dusk Pro, they sync between your devices through your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage; we can't see them. "Sunset to sunrise" schedules use your device's time zone, never your location.

Purchases

Dusk Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Problem reports

"Report a problem" opens an email in your own mail app, filled in with the site's address and your Dusk settings so we can reproduce it. You can read and edit it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use reports only to fix Dusk and don't share them.

Children

Dusk collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
